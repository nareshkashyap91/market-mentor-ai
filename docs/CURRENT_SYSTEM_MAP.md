## 📊 Summary of System Capabilities & Constraints

- **Strengths:** Fully modular, robust unit test coverage across 10+ sub-engines, zero-regression test runner (`test_master_acceptance.py`), zero-cost Vercel deployment, active 2-way Telegram interactive listener daemon.
- **Current Limitations to Address in V3.0:**
  1. **Direct Telegram Alerts:** Strategies currently broadcast directly rather than passing through a centralized **Trade Quality Gate** and **Confluence Engine**.
  2. **Data Quality Status:** Data lacks explicit metadata tags (`LIVE`, `FRESH`, `STALE`, `MISSING`, `INVALID`, `CONFLICTING`).
  3. **Regime Types:** Limited to 3 regimes (`BULLISH`, `BEARISH`, `SIDEWAYS`). Needs expansion to 10 regime types.
  4. **Language Terminology:** Uses terms like "Smart Money Certainty" and hardcoded "78% Win Probability". V3.0 requires probabilistic terminology (`POSITIONING PROXY`, `MODEL_ESTIMATED_POP`).
  5. **Market Structure:** Lacks formal HTF/LTF market structure tracking ($\text{BOS}$, $\text{CHOCH}$, $\text{HH}$, $\text{HL}$, $\text{LH}$, $\text{LL}$).
  6. **UI Layout:** Needs prop-trading terminal redesign (Trader Command Center) with information hierarchy and low cognitive load.
